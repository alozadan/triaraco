Our Story

Decades of experience managing corporate technology.

Triara Co. is based in Houston, Texas. Our leadership built its foundation managing corporate technology at scale — since 1993, administering and leasing computer equipment for some of the world's largest brands, including Volkswagen, Audi, and Daimler, across contracts covering more than 5,000 units.

We bring that same discipline to environmental recycling — making sure technology, batteries, and other sensitive materials are handled responsibly, not just discarded.
